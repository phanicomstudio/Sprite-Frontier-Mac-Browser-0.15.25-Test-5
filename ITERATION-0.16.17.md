# Sprite Frontier 0.16.17 — automatic normal relief

November 1, 2026. Supersedes the pending automatic-normal status in 0.16.16. Defaults are enabled for world material images, character/prop/item sprites, room furniture/artwork and model albedo without an explicit glTF normal assignment. UI, portraits, sky rendering and procedural/emissive effects keep their existing paths. Original files, map schema, sprite tilt/proportions, frame crops, muzzle calibration, collision and live light/shadow settings are unchanged.

## What is prepared

The pinned local Sprite Fusion generator estimates normals from painted detail/silhouette. The existing static normal-lighting implementation prepares mild front-facing relief once (ambient 0.4, strength 0.6, direction 0/0/1). The resulting native-size image enters the original radius-lighting, fog and shadow paths. This is **cached image-space relief**, not a new per-pixel moving-light/TBN shader. Custom sprite bindings and room/furniture normals retain their existing directional controls; glTF normalTexture materials are left untouched rather than double-processed. No additional GPU normal filter is installed for the automatic default.

All 116 eligible packaged images have shared normal PNGs and native-size relief art. Relief WebP is lossless, verified against the generated PNG including transparent RGB. All 116 output dimensions and complete alpha planes were also checked against their original artwork. Normal detail is capped at a 512-pixel side; displayed artwork remains native-size. Uniform sheet cells are isolated. Opaque tiling materials use wrapped neighbours, and transparent normal padding is flattened before scaling to avoid invalid normals. Source hashes prevent stale prepared artwork when an existing asset is replaced.

## Future artwork and performance

Normal preparation is automatic in the supported world-image/sprite/material/model import paths. New artwork uses one local worker pipeline, with persistent content/recipe/layout-keyed reuse. Room/model readiness includes material preparation; sprites keep original art while pending. The automatic source guard is 8,192 pixels per side and 16,777,216 pixels total; existing importer-specific limits still apply. Uniform sprite cell metadata must match the sheet. Failed/unreadable/unsupported art retains its original appearance and records a workbench issue.

The additional automatic sprite-image cache is capped at 256 MiB on devices reporting at least 8 GB memory, otherwise 128 MiB. Cold images may be evicted; a full hot set retains original art instead of exceeding the cap. Those figures count retained RGBA reservations, not total browser/driver/transient memory. Prepared original GPU copies are retired during handoff. The separate custom-normal cache remains 64 MiB. Persistent derived PNG storage is limited to 16 records of at most 8 MiB encoded data each; oversized records remain session-only. One asset pipeline is bounded by a 90-second abort. No normal generation or full-size shading runs in the draw traversal, and no per-frame diagnostics are logged.

Warm wilderness checks in first-person, third-person and isometric modes observed one normal renderer submission per frame, no recurring generation or image preparation, and bounded cache use. Measured GPU-completed draw medians were approximately 5–6 ms both enabled and disabled on this workstation. These are draw-only measurements, not a universal FPS/zero-cost guarantee; first asset preparation, decoding and upload still cost time. Dithering reuse and the existing lighting/projectile regression gates remain in place.

## Authoring and export

Use the usual sprite, item, material or model importer. No separate normal-generation step is needed for defaults. Open Environment → Sprite normals for the preparation snapshot/issues or to author a custom sprite override. A custom normal must be applied through its existing owner; imported GLB normal assignments are preserved.

Playable exports now retain the local generator/payload and complete vendor notices/provenance, plus the generated normal/relief bank. Editor-only workbenches remain omitted and the exact native export-inventory guard is retained. Newly imported art can prepare on a fresh installation; no external Rust installation or network service is required.

Developer rebuild: run `node tools/bake-automatic-normals.cjs` with Playwright/Chrome available, then `python tools/compress-automatic-normals.py` with Pillow. Original artwork is never overwritten. The generated manifest is checked against source hashes. When adding a new specialized renderer, route world images through the shared owner rather than intercepting general drawImage calls used by UI and skies.
