# Sprite Frontier 0.16.19 — projectiles always emit light

November 1, 2026. The user explicitly removed optional projectile lighting. This supersedes 0.16.15's map checkbox and all earlier per-weapon opt-out behavior. Historical comments and the retired handler are retained in SOURCE-HISTORY/projectile-switch-0.16.15.txt and Git.

Map validation now always enables projectile emission and upgrades old zero brightness to a positive default. Weapon override validation does the same. Every real shot copies validated positive light parameters at creation, including shots from weapons with no override. The live frame owner also enforces the invariant against stale unvalidated runtime/map/save values. Map changes, entering the editor, F2 playtesting and save/reload cannot toggle emission off.

The map checkbox and weapon off choice are removed. The selected projectile source's generic enabled control is hidden; validation remains authoritative even if stale input attempts to uncheck it. Color, positive brightness, radius, falloff and flicker remain configurable. Other map/player/placed-light settings, normals, fog, collision and the separate shadow systems retain their owners. General map lighting being off does not prevent a live projectile-light snapshot; it preserves that map's full-bright ambient and excludes other disabled sources. Full ambient can naturally mask diffuse light contrast; there is no emission opt-out.

Projectile candidates take priority over decorative/player/item lights and may use all eight existing shader slots, independent of a smaller decorative-light budget. More than eight simultaneous projectiles retain the nearest eight active sources; every projectile remains configured to emit, but the established GPU budget is preserved. Foreign-cell and destroyed/removed shots do not leave lights behind. Physical room-volume light occlusion is unchanged.

Validation: mandatory positive defaults and legacy JSON migration; real shot factories with map/weapon off and zero overrides; three camera modes; absent disable controls; actual map selections, editor/playtest and project reload; eight-slot priority under a one-light decorative budget; real live-normal GPU direction response and projectile emission; modern connected door transfers; generated runtime/save/load; existing lighting/weapon/core suites and function comments. Full Windows package acceptance and archive integrity accompany delivery.
