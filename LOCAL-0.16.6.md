# Local Windows version — 0.16.6

Extract the complete Windows ZIP and open Launch Sprite Frontier.cmd or Sprite Frontier.exe. Keep all runtime files and resources beside the executable. The existing desktop save profile is retained; older releases remain intact.

Paired-door arrivals now start about five feet out on your remembered approach side and extend slightly farther when the third-person camera needs clear space. They face outward and reject blocked/unsafe destinations instead of spawning behind the door.

Choose Isometric Textured Wilderness, enter Playtest with F2, click to activate, then use the wheel to zoom much closer. WASD moves, mouse aims, F/left click fires, Space draws/holsters and E jumps. A subtle ground contact blob shrinks/lightens during the jump and returns on landing. First-person zoom remains disabled.

See ITERATION-0.16.6.md and RELEASE-VALIDATION-0.16.6.json for behavior, performance budgets and verification.
